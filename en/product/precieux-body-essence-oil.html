COCODEMER Precieux Body Essence Oil

Care for dry and sensitive skin

HIGHLIGHT

Smooth, soft body care with 100% naturally derived oils

# Sinks in with a silky lightness and charges skin with lasting moisture

Absorbs the moment it touches the skin, finishing fresh and weightless. A veil of moisture then seals the surface, minimising water loss and keeping skin hydrated for longer.

# Blocks moisture evaporation so hydration lasts

Nourishing oils form a protective veil that shields rough, sensitised skin and keeps moisture from escaping.

# A brightening effect for healthy, luminous skin

Fills the skin with moisture for a smooth, lustrous and soft texture.

PRODUCT FEATURES
1. 0% synthetic oils

100% naturally derived ingredients
Coconut, meadowfoam seed, olive, argan, almond seed, green tea seed, macadamia and camellia seed. All 100% naturally derived, all skin-friendly, absorbing quickly and finishing fresh — so they suit every skin, from very dry to oily.

2. A silky finish without greasiness

A soft, fresh formula, without the heaviness and stickiness typical of natural oils. It forms a protective veil that shields rough, sensitised skin, and its anti-dry action leaves the surface soft and smooth.

TECHNOLOGY

L-CODEMAR ELIXIR™

COCODEMER's proprietary technology.
Our multi-layer nano-gel emulsion encapsulation technology holds the core actives — coconut water, phytosterols, 20 amino acids and honey extract — in a stable complex. That complex is then built into a skin-friendly liposomal form that mirrors the structure of skin itself, for the most efficient delivery into the skin.
L-Codemar Elixir™ strengthens the bonds between skin cells. It restores a damaged barrier and forms a strong protective film, so hydration holds for longer.

HOW TO USE

How To Use

  1. Turn the pump anticlockwise and press down.
  2. After showering, while skin is still slightly damp, dispense an appropriate amount and massage evenly over the body.
  3. Reapply as needed to drier areas, or where you would like the scent to linger.
