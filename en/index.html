BRAND

Connect Code of intrinsic Merit

The name COCODEMER carries two meanings at once: the Coco-de-Mer of the Seychelles, and Connect Code of intrinsic Merit — the code that connects intrinsic worth.

ORIGIN

A material that begins in the Seychelles

The Coco-de-Mer grows only in the Seychelles and bears the largest seed in the world. It takes years to ripen, in an environment that has barely changed — and it is where this brand starts.

Modelled on that seed, which is rich in naturally occurring amino acids, we built L-CODEMAR ELIXIR™ — a proprietary technology on a botanical base.

Brand Story
TECHNOLOGY

Air-in System™

The formulation technology used in Aqua Quick Bubble Mask, currently on sale. Micro-bubble particles carry more surface area than the same volume of liquid, widening the contact area with the skin so the actives absorb rather than sit on top.

THREE CHARACTERISTICS

Micro-bubbles that settle into the contours of the skin

Dense micro-bubbles settle into the contours of the skin, delivering moisture and actives quickly and evenly. Absorbed as applied, with no rinsing step, it fits into a routine without interrupting it.

Each of the three stages — Reset, Boost and Keep — has a technology of its own. Those still in development will be published with their test data when the products carrying them launch.

From Bespoke Formulation Skin Code
