Ascorbyl Tetraisopalmitate

About

Ascorbyl Tetraisopalmitate is a tetraester of ascorbic acid and isopalmitic acid. It is created through a controlled enzymatic reaction between ascorbic acid — better known as vitamin C — and the fatty acid isopalmitic acid.

Benefits
  • Boosts collagen synthesis
  • Lifts a dull complexion and counters melanin production.
  • Outstanding antioxidant performance.
In depth

This ingredient is created through a controlled enzymatic reaction between ascorbic acid — commonly known as vitamin C — and the fatty acid isopalmitic acid. It is far more stable than vitamin C against the outside environment (heat, light, oxygen in the air and pH), so it can be delivered into the body reliably, and among the ascorbic acid derivatives it is rated one of the most stable of all. Ascorbyl Tetraisopalmitate offers the same benefits as vitamin C: it scavenges free radicals and prevents peroxidation of the lipid layer, inhibits the enzymes behind melanin production and promotes collagen synthesis. Research has also shown that it reduces the melanin already present in the skin, confirming its brightening effect.

Brightening · Cs

Carnosine

About

Carnosine is a molecule made up of two amino acids found naturally in the body, β-alanine and histidine. It closely resembles the free amino groups that glycation targets, and can react with glycation in their place. Carnosine is produced by an enzymatic process — hydrazine-free, avoiding the hazards of chemical synthesis.

Benefits
  • Protects the skin
  • Effectively reduces wrinkles and increases firmness.
  • Delivers antioxidant action, supports collagen synthesis and helps prevent ageing.
In depth

Carnosine is a celebrated anti-ageing ingredient, particularly in food, with exceptional antioxidant power: it clears free radicals from the body and blocks the ageing that oxidation causes. Alongside oxidation there is a second reaction behind ageing — glycation. On the skin, glycation shows itself as deep lines and fine wrinkles, and age-related skin ageing is largely the result of skin collagen cross-linking through glycation and proteins becoming denatured. Carnosine prevents that protein damage and clears away proteins that have already been damaged, and its anti-ageing effect on the skin has been demonstrated. It can also defend cells and tissue against formaldehyde toxicity and the accelerated ageing it brings.

Benefits

In formulation it also reduces viscosity, and is widely used as a solvent for other ingredients.

Niacinamide

About

Listed by Korea's Ministry of Food and Drug Safety (MFDS) as a functional (approved) active for brightening, Niacinamide is the water-soluble vitamin B3, also known as vitamin PP.

Benefits
  • Helps brighten the skin.
  • Helps keep the complexion clear and radiant.
In depth

Melanin is the collective name for the black to brown pigments present in the skin, eyes and other tissues of many animals. It exists mainly as melanoprotein, bound strongly to globulin; it does not dissolve in water, but does dissolve in alkaline solutions and in concentrated sulphuric acid. Melanin blocks ultraviolet light above a certain level, so it helps maintain the skin's temperature and protects it from UV. The amount of melanin present is also what determines skin colour.
Melanin is produced inside a specialised structure called the melanosome — a small sac-like body formed within the cytoplasm of the melanocyte. Melanocytes extend projections carrying melanosomes to pass melanin on to neighbouring cells such as keratinocytes; where a melanosome contains phenols such as tyrosine together with their oxidising enzymes, it may instead store the melanin it has formed inside itself. The melanosomes produced are transferred into the epidermal cells, and this is what darkens the skin and hair.

Tranexamic Acid

About

Tranexamic Acid is a synthetic amino acid — a crystalline compound derived from the amino acid lysine — and is used above all to brighten the skin.

Benefits
  • Brightens the skin and improves blemishes
  • Soothes the skin and calms inflammation
  • Tones the skin with an astringent action
In depth

Melanin is produced to protect the skin, but when too much of it forms, dark spots and pigmentation surface. Its anti-inflammatory, anti-allergic and haemostatic properties have long been used in medicine, where it is widely prescribed as a haemostatic agent and for tonsillitis, eczema and urticaria.

Adenosine

About

Adenosine is a purine nucleoside in which adenine, one of the nucleobases that make up DNA, is joined to the five-carbon sugar ribose by a β-N9-glycosidic bond. One of the body's own substances, it binds to adenosine receptors within the cell to promote collagen formation and support the skin. Safe and naturally occurring, Adenosine is a widely used functional (approved) active for wrinkle improvement.

Benefits
  • Restores firmness to skin slackened by age and to fine lines
  • Helps plump uneven skin and keep it firm and resilient.
  • Promotes collagen production and inhibits its breakdown
In depth

As the skin gradually ages, production of the collagen and elastin that make up the skin tissue falls away, and even the collagen and elastin already there stop performing as they should. Firmness drops, the tissue slackens and the surface of the skin becomes uneven — and that is what we call a "wrinkle".
Wrinkles, however, cannot be put down to skin ageing alone. External factors such as photoageing from UV exposure play their part, so they are not purely a matter of natural ageing, and how well the skin is cared for makes a real difference from one person to the next.
Adenosine promotes the production of collagen and elastin to improve firmness, lifting slackened skin and filling out the uneven surface.

About

Isotretinoin is a fat-soluble vitamin A derivative that suppresses sebum secretion from the sebaceous glands, preventing acne bacteria from multiplying.

Benefits
  • Reduces the inflammatory response.

In depth

Isotretinoin belongs to the retinoid family and is recognised as the most effective agent for regulating sebum secretion. A synthetic compound derived from fat-soluble vitamin A, it regulates the proliferation and differentiation of cells to reduce the size of the sebaceous glands, and suppresses both sebum production and the secretion of sebum from those glands, thereby reducing acne bacteria — the mechanism behind its use in acne treatment. Beyond this, Isotretinoin helps interrupt the other pathways through which acne develops, including abnormal keratinisation, the inflammatory response and bacterial proliferation.

About

Spirulina

About

Spirulina is a thin-walled multicellular organism and one of the oldest algae on Earth. Its principal constituents include water-soluble dietary fibre, carbohydrates, protein, gamma-linolenic acid and antioxidant enzymes.

Benefits
  • Helps shield the skin from UV.
  • Helps increase collagen production and dermal density.
  • Outstanding for sebum control and antibacterial action.
In depth

Spirulina has drawn attention as a multifunctional cosmetic active. What makes it so beneficial for skin health is its rich nutritional profile — protein, essential fatty acids, vitamins and minerals. The prostaglandins in Spirulina inhibit platelet aggregation and improve circulation, and it has been reported to act against inflammation and to contain anti-allergic substances that suppress the immediate allergic response to both immunological and non-immunological stimuli while inhibiting the release of histamine and TNF-α. Because it absorbs UVB, it also works as a biological sunscreen rather than a physical or chemical one, and its viscous polysaccharide mucilage eases the movement of cells across the surface of the skin, providing a structure in which skin regeneration can take place readily.

Tocotrienols

About

Tocotrienols are one of the derivative families of vitamin E and, like tocopherols, comprise four forms: alpha, beta, gamma and delta. In cosmetics they serve as an antioxidant and as a skincare active, reducing transepidermal water loss and acting as a protective barrier for the skin.

Benefits
  • As an antioxidant, prevents rancidity and improves a product's stability.
  • Forms a moisture barrier that helps prevent transepidermal water loss.
  • Helps protect the skin from sunlight.
