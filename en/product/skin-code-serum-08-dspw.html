Skin coping with several concerns at once, day after day

ACTIVE CODE

Key Active Code

Moisturising — blocks moisture loss, strengthens the skin barrier, improves skin texture

＊Sh Sodium Hyaluronate — High-Molecular-Weight Hyaluronic Acid
: One of the skin's natural moisturising agents. It draws in up to several hundred times its own weight in water, helping keep skin feeling hydrated.
* Bt Betaine — Moisturising Amino Acid Derivative
: Derived from sugar beet, it helps limit moisture loss and supports the skin's protective veil.
* Rf Raffinose — Beet-Derived Oligosaccharide Humectant
: A natural moisturising agent whose structure attracts water, helping prevent moisture from evaporating.
* Pn Panthenol — Moisturising Vitamin
: An alcohol analogue of Vitamin B5 that helps moisturise and condition the skin.
*Refers to the properties of the raw ingredients only.

Whitening — antioxidant, hyperpigmentation improvement, brightening effect

＊Na Niacinamide — brightening functional ingredient
: A derivative of water-soluble Vt.B3 (niacin). It inhibits tyrosinase and blocks melanin formation to suppress pigmentation, and it reduces the substances behind collagen oxidation — brightening the skin tone and helping prevent dark spots.
* CS Carnosine — anti-glycation peptide from the wing muscle of migratory birds
: A powerful antioxidant. It clears free radicals from the body and halts the ageing caused by oxidation.
* Ct Creatine — cellular energy booster
: A reservoir of energy for the skin. It helps repair cells and supplies them with fresh energy, stimulating collagen production to improve elasticity and counter ageing.
*Refers to the properties of the raw ingredients only.

Anti Wrinkles — wrinkle improvement, greater elasticity, anti-ageing, a renewed turnover cycle, skin protection and regeneration

＊Ad Adenosine — wrinkle improvement functional ingredient
: In the dermis it promotes DNA and protein synthesis and drives fibroblast proliferation, promoting collagen and elastin synthesis.
* Cn Ceramide NP — a lipid of the skin barrier itself
: Close in composition to the barrier, it rebuilds the cell structure of a broken skin barrier and improves how the barrier works.
* Ah Acetyl hexapeptide-8 — firming lifting peptide
: It restores elasticity to slackened skin and fills uneven contours, improving both firmness and wrinkles.
* Pp Palmitoyl Pentapeptide-4 — collagen peptide
: More effective on wrinkles than retinol, and so well matched to the skin that it causes no irritation. It stimulates collagen and elastin production in the dermis, and its small molecules speed recovery.
* Sd Sodium DNA — salmon-derived DNA derivative
: Skin-friendly, it places no strain on the skin and supports regeneration.
*Refers to the properties of the raw ingredients only.

Sensitive — prevents blemishes, relieves marks, strengthens the skin barrier, regenerates and repairs, soothes

＊Ca Centella asiatica extract — Centella Asiatica Extract
: Madecassic acid heals inflammation and works as an anti-inflammatory, soothing and wound-healing agent, helping to suppress inflammation in acne-prone skin.
* Ab Aloe barbadensis leaf extract — Aloe Vera Leaf Extract
: An anthraquinone derivative, effective against inflammation and suppuration, it calms skin flushed red by UV or irritation and helps protect it.
* As Asiaticoside — inflammation relief
: Soothes and relieves skin worn down by outside irritation, helping to keep it healthy.
*Relates to the properties of the raw ingredients only.

RECOMMENDED CARE

Your Care Routine

Cleansing care

1. Clair Blanc Cleansing Cream : a deep cleanse that brings nourishment and moisture to dry skin
2. Clair Blanc Cleansing Foam : fine bubbles for a thorough cleanse and a comfortably moist finish
3. Pat away any water left after cleansing — skin that flushes easily needs as little friction as possible

Skincare

1.Meilleur Facial Toner : gives skin ample moisture to balance oil and water
(cotton pads not recommended)
2. Skin Code Serum DSPW : the serum matched to your skin, helping strengthen the skin barrier
3. Meilleur Facial Emulsion : supplies the oil your skin lacks to balance oil and water

Sun Care

Meilleur UV Sun Protecter : apply thoroughly, about a coin-sized amount, 30 minutes before heading out. Reapply every three hours.

HOW TO USE

How To Use

Pump the dropper, then smooth a small amount gently over the whole face.
