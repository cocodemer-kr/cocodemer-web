INTRODUCTION

A line that starts from the material

COCODEMER BiO does not begin with the product.
We secure a material we can work with, then find where it works best. Where our general skincare is designed around the order of use, BiO starts from the properties of the material itself. We first establish the concentration and the vehicle in which a material actually means something, and only then decide what form the product takes.
OWN MATERIALS

Our own materials

We start from materials we have sourced and verified ourselves. Securing the material comes before planning the finished product.

CONCENTRATION

Designed around concentration

We first establish the concentration range at which a material is meaningful, then choose a formulation that can hold those conditions.

A LINE OF ITS OWN

A line of its own

It will be developed separately from the general skincare system, as a BiO lineup in its own right.

※ The composition of the line and its launch timing are not yet fixed. For material proposals and joint-development enquiries, please use the B2B enquiry form.
