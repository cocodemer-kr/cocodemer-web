HIGHLIGHT

A high-end skin solution that brightens the complexion with skin's own natural health.

# 20% Pure Vitamin C

20% pure Vitamin C, stabilised by an anhydrous process that overcomes the usual limitations of Vitamin C, is blended with Pentide-C, a bio-derived material pairing Vitamin C with peptides. Together they leave skin clear, fresh and luminous.

PRODUCT FEATURES
1. 'Pure Vitamin C', a powerful antioxidant, for a radiant complexion and an even tone

'20% pure Vitamin C', stabilised by an anhydrous process that overcomes the shortcomings of Vitamin C, is blended with 'Pentide-C', a bio-material pairing Vitamin C with peptides. Together they activate the skin's own vitality, adding radiant energy from within for skin that looks clear, fresh and luminous.

2. Hydrating, soothing care for skin that feels firm and smooth

Provitamin B5 Panthenol and apricot kernel oil deliver moisture and nourishment quickly, leaving skin hydrated, firm and healthy.

3. A silky texture, dense and supple

Silky and highly concentrated under the fingertips, the cream sinks in slowly and evenly, leaving skin soft and supple.

ACTIVE CODE
Snow VC exclusive stabilisation technology
20% pure Vitamin C, fermented moisturising ingredients, bio-lipids

An anhydrous process with 0% purified water double-stabilises 20% pure Vitamin C with fermented moisturising ingredients and biolipids, for a true antioxidant solution. Shielding the active prevents discolouration, and the ingredients travel gently deep into the skin, unaffected by pH. ※ Applies patented technology for a pure Vitamin C cosmetic composition and its method of use (Patent no. KR 10-2020-0097988 / International PCT/KR2021/010279)

Pentide-C bio material
Ascorbyl Methylcarbonyl Pentapeptide-72 Tri-t-Butyl Tryptophanamide

A bio-material that pairs Vitamin C with peptides penetrates deeply with little irritation, bringing skin to its healthiest, most vital condition. It carries the actives far into the skin and feeds tired, lacklustre skin, helping it look clear and firm.
