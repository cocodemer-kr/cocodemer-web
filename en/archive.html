HERITAGE

We read the skin,
and made what matched it.

COCODEMER began by measuring skin
and compounding a product for each customer.

That work led to 17 finished products,
and later to Skin Code, an online version of the same reading.

This page holds the products and prescriptions
left from that work,
and the record of how COCODEMER has read skin.

01 · CUSTOMIZED

It started with
one person’s skin.

We did not pick the product first.

A skin analyser in the store photographed the face and returned
over two hundred readings — redness on the forehead, oil on the nose,
pigment under the eyes and more.

After reading the skin that day
we compounded the serum and cream for that customer.

Compounding worked from four criteria for the serum
and three for the cream,
and changed with the state of the skin.

The product itself
came out different for each customer.

On a return visit a month later
we measured the skin again.

If the skin had changed,
we did not repeat the earlier formula.

We compounded again
for the skin in front of us.

NOTE

The four serum and three cream figures are compounding criteria, not a product count.
The per-customer compounding service is no longer offered.

03 · SKIN CODE

The same reading,
outside the store.

So that people who could not visit a store
could still read their own skin the same way,

we built Skin Code Solution.

Dry / Oily
Sensitive / Resistant
Pigmented / Non-pigmented
Wrinkle / Elasticity

The four axes combine into
sixteen skin types.

After an online skin test identified the type,
a serum was compounded to that type’s prescription
and shipped to the customer.
