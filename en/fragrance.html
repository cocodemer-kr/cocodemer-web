FRAGRANCE

One Forest, Multiple States

INTRODUCTION

One forest. Many moments.

Not a collection of perfumes,
but a record of a forest captured through scent. Morning light. After footsteps fade. Sea air moving through trees. The same forest, never the same state. COCODEMER does not create fragrance for someone — it creates fragrance for a state of being.
FIRST CONTACT

Seychelles Memory

Warmth on skin. Air and wood at the moment of entry.

SILENCE

No Man's Forest

After presence disappears, the forest sharpens.

MOVEMENT

Sea Breeze Petals

Wind shifts the boundary. Breath moves through leaves.

ORIGIN

The Coco de Mer palm forest, Seychelles

A real forest, and the starting point of the brand's memory. Home to the world's largest seed — a place of isolation and scale. Primal. Untouched. Sacred.

Not a place to visit, but a place to remain — and that staying is what the fragrance translates.

PRASLIN ISLAND, SEYCHELLES · 4°19′S 55°44′E
THE COLLECTION

Three states

01 · First Contact

Seychelles Memory

Signature Scent

The moment the forest first wrapped the skin.

Dry coconut. Wood without sweetness, its grain settling on the skin. Jasmine and violet sit barely present, like plants hidden deep inside the forest, while green notes carry the moisture of leaves and air.

TOPCoconut
MIDDLEViolet, Jasmine, Green notes
BASEAmber, Musk, Sandalwood, Cedarwood, Patchouli
02 · Silence

No Man's Forest

Second State

After people leave, the forest remains.

Light dims and sound falls away, yet the trees, the air and the scent only sharpen. There is no opening here — no distraction. The scent begins where it stays: iris and eucalyptus emerge immediately, and the base is present from the first moment.

TOP(None) — deliberately withheld
MIDDLEIris, Eucalyptus, Green notes
BASEAmber, Musk, Sandalwood, Cedarwood, Vanilla
03 · Movement

Sea Breeze Petals

Third State

Sea air brushes the forest's edge, and petals move quietly through it.

Lime and lemon open the air — fresh, restrained. Green notes, muguet, neroli and eucalyptus trace a quiet motion between forest and sea.

TOPLime, Lemon
MIDDLEGreen notes, Muguet, Neroli, Eucalyptus
BASEMusk, Amber, Cedarwood
PRODUCT CONFIGURATION

Two forms

Eau de Parfum

50ml
  • Alcohol-based, in the traditional eau de parfum form
  • Balanced diffusion and wear
  • Moves through space and leaves a trace

Solid Perfume

6g
  • Alcohol-free solid form
  • Applied directly to skin, where it stays close
  • Portable, and reapplied whenever you like
  • Intimate — perceived only at close distance
