A hand cream that leaves skin moisturised and smooth

HIGHLIGHT

A silky-touch hand cream with a fresh, moisturising finish

# A softening effect that leaves skin hydrated and supple

Protects the skin from fine dust and harmful elements, keeping hands beautiful and lustrous.

# Strengthens a barrier roughened by external stress

Ceramide NP, close in structure to the skin's own barrier components, reorders the cell structure of a broken-down barrier and improves the way that barrier is strengthened.

# Sinks in with a silky lightness and charges skin with lasting moisture

A veil of moisture formed by a range of hydrating ingredients keeps water loss to a minimum and holds hydration for longer.

PRODUCT FEATURES
1.
Skin-fit membrane technology

Plant-derived humectants (Salvia Officinalis (Sage) Leaf Extract, Rosmarinus Officinalis (Rosemary) Leaf Extract and others) and softening oils (Carthamus Tinctorius (Safflower) Seed Oil, Oenothera Biennis (Evening Primrose) Oil, Olea Europaea (Olive) Fruit Oil, Camellia Sinensis Seed Oil and others) are stabilised in a cationic liquid-crystal emulsion, for a soft, non-sticky veil of moisture that stays close to the skin.

2.
Moisture barrier technology

Moisture-barrier technology forms a shield against fine dust and airborne pollutants. Skin gains a non-sticky luminosity, and resilient nourishment fills it from within for a smooth finish.

3.
Moisture that sits close to the skin, never sticky

On rough hands, moisture bursts on contact — a water-bomb finish that leaves skin healthy and hydrated, never sticky.

TECHNOLOGY

L-CODEMAR ELIXIR™

COCODEMER's proprietary technology.
Our multi-layer nano-gel emulsion encapsulation technology holds the core actives — coconut water, phytosterols, 20 amino acids and honey extract — in a stable complex. That complex is then built into a skin-friendly liposomal form that mirrors the structure of skin itself, for the most efficient delivery into the skin.
L-Codemar Elixir™ strengthens the bonds between skin cells. It restores a damaged barrier and forms a strong protective film, so hydration holds for longer.

HOW TO USE

How To Use

After washing your hands, take a suitable amount and work it evenly into dry skin until absorbed.
