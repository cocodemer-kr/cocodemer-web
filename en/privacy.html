COCODEMER CO., LTD. (the "Company") complies with the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea and related legislation, and publishes this Privacy Policy to protect the personal data of its users. This is an English translation provided for convenience; in the event of any discrepancy, the Korean version prevails.

1. What we collect, and how

This website (www.cocodemer.co.kr) provides no membership registration, login or online payment function, and does not collect personal data directly. Retail purchases take place on Naver Smart Store, where personal data is processed under that platform's own policy.

Where a user voluntarily contacts us by email or telephone, we may collect a name (or company name), email address, contact number and the content of the inquiry.

2. Purpose of use

Responding to inquiries and providing consultation; negotiating and performing trade and distribution partnership agreements.

3. Retention period

Personal data is destroyed without delay once the purpose of its collection has been achieved, except where retention is required by law (records of contracts or withdrawal of subscription: 5 years; records of payment and supply of goods: 5 years; records of consumer complaints or dispute resolution: 3 years — Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce).

4. Disclosure to third parties

The Company does not provide users' personal data to third parties, except where required by law or upon a lawful request by an investigative authority.

5. Destruction of data

Data held in electronic form is permanently deleted by a method that prevents recovery; printed material is shredded or incinerated.

6. User rights

Users may at any time request access to, correction of, deletion of, or suspension of processing of their personal data. The Company will act without delay in accordance with applicable law.

7. Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and does not transmit user-identifying information to Company servers.
