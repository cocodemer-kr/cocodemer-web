WHAT WE OFFER

How to carry COCODEMER

COCODEMER develops and produces its own brand as a registered cosmetics responsible-distributor. There are four ways to carry the brand or build with us, and they can be combined to suit your market and channel.

01
Finished-goods export

COCODEMER-branded products supplied as they are. Aqua Quick Bubble Mask is available now; the rest of the Reset · Boost · Keep line and the fragrance collection follow.

02
Country-exclusive distribution

Exclusive rights to the brand in a given country or region. Terms are set together, against your channel plan and volume targets.

03
Fragrance licence · co-development

Carrying the fragrance collection as a line of its own, or developing it jointly with a local partner. Discussions start from the two formats: 50ml eau de parfum and 6g solid perfume.

04
OEM · ODM

Production under your own brand, drawing on our Korean manufacturing network and the formulation assets we have built — L-CODEMAR ELIXIR™, the CSTI 16-type framework, and the Skin Code Serum formulations.
