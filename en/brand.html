BRAND

Looking at the skin first.
COCODEMER started there.

We started from a plain fact:
skin differs from person to person.

We measured it,read it,and made what matched it.

DESIGNED FOR THE SKIN

We read the differences first.

  • Dryness and oil,
  • sensitivity and resistance,
  • differences in pigment,
  • wrinkles and elasticity.

Skin differs from person to person,
and the same skin changes over time.

COCODEMER did not try to fit one product
to every kind of skin.

THE NAME

A name that came from the coco de mer

The name COCODEMER comes from
the coco de mer of the Seychelles.

The tree bears the largest seed in the plant kingdom,
and it gave the brand not only its name
but the starting point of our early ingredient research.

CONNECTING CODES TO APPROACH
THE INTRINSIC MERIT

Reading the traits that make each skin different,
and finding the answer that fits it.

Reading the 'code' of each skin
and finding the merit already in it —
that idea sits inside the name.

FROM CUSTOMIZED TO SKIN CODE

We read the skin,
then made it differently.

COCODEMER started as a custom cosmetics service:
we measured skin and compounded to those differences,
one customer at a time.

That work led to 17 finished products
for everyday use alongside the compounded ones,

and later, through Skin Code Solution,
the same reading was carried outside the store.

View HERITAGE

WHAT REMAINS

The method stopped.
The standard stayed.

We no longer run
the per-customer compounding service.

Before we make a product, we still start
from the skin and what it needs.

TODAY

COCODEMER now designs skincare
in three stages.

Prepare first,

add what is needed,

Hold that condition longer.

  1. 01

    RESET

    Prepare the skin first

  2. 02

    BOOST

    Add what the skin needs

  3. 03

    KEEP

    Hold that condition longer.

What was once made differently for each person
is now made in the stages a skin condition calls for.

View the skincare system

THE NEXT COCODEMER

Fewer products,
more reasons to trust them.

COCODEMER will keep making skincare
that starts from skin condition and purpose
rather than from the product.

We build up the tests and data each product needs,
and add the evidence one piece at a time.

INGREDIENT LEGACY

Ingredient research that began
with the brand name

L-CODEMAR ELIXIR™, drawn from the coco de mer seed,
is a proprietary COCODEMER material

developed while we ran the custom cosmetics service.

It was used in the finished lineup of that period,
and the ingredient and formulation records are still kept.

NOTE

L-CODEMAR ELIXIR™ is not used in
Aqua Quick Bubble Mask, the product currently on sale.
