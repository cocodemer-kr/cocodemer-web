COCODEMER Meilleur Infusion Nourishing Cream

A dual-function nourishing cream: deep moisture and nourishment, with brightening and wrinkle improvement

HIGHLIGHT

An energy booster cream that helps restore the skin's vitality

# Firmness care that builds from within

Generous nourishment sinks deep into the skin, leaving it healthy and resilient.

# A finish packed with moisture and nourishment

Nourishing, moisturising ingredients form a veil of moisture inside the skin and on its surface. Tired skin improves as nourishment fills it back up and the skin recovers.

# A dense, cushiony velvet texture

It spreads soft and cushioned, settling close to the skin. The velvet texture leaves skin smooth, never sticky.

PRODUCT FEATURES
1. Hydrogel assembly technology

A composite technology — plant oils, nano-emulsions and polymer elastomer gel, fermented together — holds the actives in skin-friendly capsules and carries them efficiently into the skin, so nourishment reaches it smoothly.

2. Dual functional cosmetic for brightening and wrinkle improvement

Niacinamide inhibits melanin production to help brighten the skin, while Adenosine helps improve wrinkles.

3. Improves damaged skin tissue, with a powerful moisture barrier inside and out

Collagen & elastin peptides, active vitamin (B5), water-soluble humectants, low- and high-molecular-weight hyaluronic acid, oligosaccharides,
Acetyl Glutamine and botanical moisturising & soothing ingredients — burdock, soybean, Ficus Carica (Fig) Fruit and Centella Asiatica extracts — build a veil of moisture within the skin and on its surface,
so the surface and the moisture beneath it are cared for alike.

TECHNOLOGY

L-CODEMAR ELIXIR™

COCODEMER's proprietary technology.
Our multi-layer nano-gel emulsion encapsulation technology holds the core actives — coconut water, phytosterols, 20 amino acids and honey extract — in a stable complex. That complex is then built into a skin-friendly liposomal form that mirrors the structure of skin itself, for the most efficient delivery into the skin.
L-Codemar Elixir™ strengthens the bonds between skin cells. It restores a damaged barrier and forms a strong protective film, so hydration holds for longer.

HOW TO USE

How To Use

1. Take a suitable amount using the enclosed spatula.

2. Smooth gently from the centre of the face outwards, following the skin's texture.

3. Pat lightly to absorb.
