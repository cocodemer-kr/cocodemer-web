HIGHLIGHT

Shake and apply — the bubbles do the rest

# Air-in System

At the moment of pumping, micelles in the formula trap air and form dense bubbles. As those bubbles settle against the skin, the actives they carry are absorbed quickly.

# A daily routine with no rinsing

Nothing to apply and peel off, nothing to wash away. The formula absorbs where it lands, so it fits into a morning or an evening routine without interrupting either.

PRODUCT FEATURES
1. A no-rinse formula

Apply, let it absorb, and it is done — a 5-second mask that never sends you back to the basin. It works as a luminous make-up base by day and as nourishing night care after dark.

2. Over 100 uses per can

The 80ml can lasts more than three months at daily use, and unlike a sheet mask it leaves nothing to throw away.

3. Plant-derived surfactants

Every surfactant in the formula is plant-derived. No parabens.

KEY INGREDIENTS
Niacinamide

Certified by Korea's MFDS as a brightening functional ingredient. It curbs melanin activity, helping to soften dark spots and even out skin tone.

Adenosine

Certified by Korea's MFDS as a wrinkle-care functional ingredient. It supports collagen synthesis and contributes to firmer skin.

Triple-layer hyaluronic acid complex

Patent no. 10-2289551. Three hyaluronic acids of differing molecular weight work together, layering moisture from the surface down towards the dermal layer.

Panthenol and ectoin

Vitamin B5 panthenol conditions the skin barrier, while ectoin — found in Egypt's salt lakes — forms a natural protective film against dryness and external stress. Ectoin is rated EWG grade 1.

Jeju prickly pear cactus stem extract

The trehalose that lets this cactus hold water in the desert does the same on skin, keeping moisture where it is.

HOW TO USE

① Shake the can thoroughly before use. ② Pump a suitable amount into the hand to form bubbles. ③ Spread gently over the whole face and let it absorb. Do not rinse. ※ On first opening the layers may have separated, so shake well before use.
