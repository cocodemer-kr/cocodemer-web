MEASUREMENT

With every return visit, the formula changed

Photographing the face in store returned over two hundred readings — redness, oil, pigment and more. Returning clients were measured again, and when the numbers moved, the formula moved with them.

CSTI is the indicator that sorts skin along four axes into sixteen types. Bespoke compounding combined bases, actives and scents into 98,319 possible formulations; the Skin Code Serums were built on that same system.

The compounding service has ended. The formulations and ingredient records are kept. Today's skincare system was designed from that experience.

BRAND

Connect Code of intrinsic Merit

The coco de mer grows only in the Seychelles. The brand takes its name from that seed — linking what the plant holds to a form the skin can take.

ORIGIN

A proprietary material from the coco de mer

The coco de mer bears the largest seed in the plant kingdom, in a forest held as UNESCO World Heritage.

L-CODEMAR ELIXIR™ reproduces the seed's amino-acid composition in plant-derived form, and was used across the 33-piece archive.

Brand Story
TECHNOLOGY

Air-in System™

The formulation technology used in Aqua Quick Bubble Mask, currently on sale. Fine bubbles are designed to sit close against the surface and contours of the skin.

THREE CHARACTERISTICS

Micro-bubbles that settle into the contours of the skin

A formulation whose dense micro-bubbles settle into the contours of the skin. Used without rinsing; 80ml gives roughly 100 or more applications.

Each of the three stages — Reset, Boost and Keep — has a technology of its own. Those still in development will be published with their test data when the products carrying them launch.
