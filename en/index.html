BRAND

Connect Code of intrinsic Merit

COCODEMER — a name meaning the countless energies nature creates — begins from a belief in inner dignity and essential worth. It exists to help each person find their own health and beauty.

ORIGIN

Vital Energy Drawn from Nature

Born of years of research, COCODEMER reads the lifestyle and the skin shaped by each person's inner and outer environment. The answer is a solution made for that skin alone.

The rare botanical materials of L-CODEMAR ELIXIR™, combined with innovative bio-technology.

Brand Story
TECHNOLOGY

L-CODEMAR ELIXIR™

Bio-technology that works as close to the skin as it can get. Our proprietary ingredient technology began with the Coco-de-Mer of the Seychelles, a UNESCO World Heritage site.

FOUR PILLARS

A Proprietary Complex Built on Four Pillars

Inside: coconut water modelled on the amino-acid-rich Coco-de-Mer, the phytosterols plants make to heal their own wounds, twenty amino acids and honey extract — together, a resilient foundation for the skin.

Skin-compatible lipid membrane research meets solubilisation technology for poorly soluble compounds. Together they carry active ingredients into the skin and keep them stable there.
