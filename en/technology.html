FORMULATION TECHNOLOGY

Ingredient and formulation, built together.

The compounding system used L-CODEMAR ELIXIR™
together with formulation work on stabilising,
carrying and texturing ingredients.

L-CODEMAR ELIXIR™

An ingredient design drawn from the coco de mer,
built on a coconut water base.

  • Coconut WaterCoconut water
  • PhytosterolPhytosterols
  • 20 Amino Acids20 amino acids
  • Honey ExtractHoney extract
01

Skin-compatible lipid membrane design

A design that combines ingredients similar to the lipids skin is made of.

02

Stabilising and carrying active ingredients

Unstable ingredients are stabilised inside a capsule structure and held in the formulation.

03

Solubilising poorly soluble ingredients

Ingredients that dissolve poorly, such as ceramides, are brought into solution within the formulation.

※ Ingredient and formulation work from the period when the custom cosmetics service ran. L-CODEMAR ELIXIR™ is not used in Aqua Quick Bubble Mask, the product currently on sale.
