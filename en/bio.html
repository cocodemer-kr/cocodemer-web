COCODEMER BIO

Another approach to skincare,
starting with materials

We study bio materials from specialist fields
and make them into products for the skin.On the same brand ground,
this opens a second axis that starts from the material.

ORIGIN · WHY BIO

COCODEMER looks at skin first.
COCODEMER BIO looks at the material first.

COCODEMER started by looking at skin, measuring it and reading it.
COCODEMER BIO started from bio materials
encountered in specialist fields. Rather than fixing the product first, we look at what a material does,
then work out the product and texture that suit it.

MATERIAL-LED APPROACH

From material to product

01 MATERIAL

Read the material.

We look first at what a specialist material does and where it could be used.

02 CONDITION

Set the conditions.

We work out the concentration, the combination and the texture in which the material stays stable.

03 FORMAT

Make the product.

We settle the form and the way it is used, then make the product.
