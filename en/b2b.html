GLOBAL B2B

Connecting K-beauty to global markets

WHAT WE DO

A brand owner and K-beauty distribution partner

COCODEMER is a licensed cosmetics responsible-distributor that develops and produces its own brand. Through distribution partnerships, we also supply K-beauty products B2B to the Middle East, Europe and Greater China.

01
Brand Sourcing

Drawing on proven Korean brands and an ODM network, we propose product line-ups matched to the buyer's channel, price band and regulatory requirements.

02
Certification & Export Documentation

We handle market-by-market registration — EU CPNP, Taiwan TFDA, China NMPA — together with export documentation including certificates of origin, ingredient lists and CPSR.

03
Export Logistics

We run small- and mid-volume export operations in house, including LCL consolidated shipping. Low-quantity, multi-item orders are welcome.

MARKETS

Key Markets

We operate distribution partnerships focused on the Middle East, Europe and Greater China (China · Taiwan),
and are preparing to enter the North American and Japanese markets.
