COCO-DE-MER

It begins with a UNESCO World Heritage site

A search for the essence of beauty, and for the worth inherent in every person. It begins with the Coco-de-Mer of the Seychelles, which retains its primordial vitality and purity to this day. Bound up with the legend of Eden and known as the goddess of love, the Coco-de-Mer is a UNESCO World Heritage species prized for both its nobility and its rarity.

Drawing on advanced dermatological science, we modelled the amino-acid-rich Coco-de-Mer to develop L-CODEMAR ELIXIR™ — a plant-based complex that restores the skin's natural health.

L-CODEMAR ELIXIR™

Bio-technology that absorbs closest to the skin

Using a singular bio-technology that carries actives as close to the skin as possible, we captured the life energy found in nature. The result is L-CODEMAR ELIXIR™.

FOUR PILLARS

Coconut Water — modelled on the Coco-de-Mer, rich in the natural amino acids long regarded as an elixir of youth.

Phytosterols — the compounds plants generate to heal their own wounds.

20 Amino Acids · Honey — builds a firm foundation for the skin.

SKIN CODE

16 skin types, 16 formulations

CSTI (COCODEMER Skin Type Indicator) sorts skin into 16 types along four axes: dry/oily, sensitive/resistant, pigmented/non-pigmented, and wrinkled/firm. The 16 Skin Code Serums are the base and active-ingredient combinations matched to those types.
