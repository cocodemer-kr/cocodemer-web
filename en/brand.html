COCODEMER

Connect Code of intrinsic Merit

The coco de mer grows only in the Seychelles.

It bears the largest seed in the plant kingdom, in a forest held as UNESCO World Heritage.

The brand takes its name from that seed. Connect Code of intrinsic Merit — linking what the plant holds to a form the skin can take.

DESIGNED FOR THE SKIN

It started with measurement

COCODEMER began by measuring one person's skin in store and compounding to those readings.

We measured the skin first and set the formula from the result. That became Designed for the skin.

L-CODEMAR ELIXIR™

A proprietary material from the coco de mer

The coco de mer seed is rich in natural amino acids. L-CODEMAR ELIXIR™ reproduces that composition in plant-derived form, and was used across the 33-piece archive.

Carried in a structure resembling the skin's own lipid membrane.

Ingredients & Technology
FROM CUSTOMIZED TO SYSTEMIZED

From customized to systemized

If every skin differs, does one product for everyone make sense? So COCODEMER started by measuring.

STUDIO

With every return visit, the formula changed

A skin analyser stood in the store. Photographing the face returned over two hundred readings — redness on the forehead, oil on the nose, pigment under the eyes. We compounded to those numbers on the spot.

A month later they came back and we measured again. When the skin had changed, the formula changed. Run the same way in Seoul and in China.

SKIN CODE

Skin sorted into sixteen

We needed a way to answer skin type online as well. Skin was divided along four axes — dry or oily, sensitive or resistant, pigmented or not, wrinkled or firm. Combined, they give sixteen types. That classification is CSTI.

Bespoke compounding combined bases, actives and scents into 98,319 possible formulations. Built on that same system, the sixteen Skin Code Serums answer the sixteen types.

SYSTEM

The compounding room closed. The records did not.

The compounding service has ended and the formulations and ingredient records are kept. The 33-piece archive is entirely sold out.

Today's system was designed from those formulations and ingredient records. Reset · Boost · Keep is the result.
