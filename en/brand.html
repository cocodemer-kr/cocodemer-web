COCODEMER

Connect Code of intrinsic Merit

The name COCODEMER carries two meanings at once.

One is the Coco-de-Mer of the Seychelles — a rare palm protected as a UNESCO World Heritage species. Its seed is where our proprietary material, L-CODEMAR ELIXIR™, began.

The other is Connect Code of intrinsic Merit — the code that connects intrinsic worth.

To begin with a rare plant, design it into a form the skin will take, and connect the two. That single sentence is what COCODEMER does.

ORIGIN

A material that begins in the Seychelles

The Coco-de-Mer grows only in the Seychelles and bears the largest seed in the world. It takes years to ripen, in an environment that has barely changed — and it is where this brand starts.

Modelled on that seed, which is rich in naturally occurring amino acids, we built L-CODEMAR ELIXIR™ — a proprietary technology on a botanical base. Multi-layer nano-gel emulsion encapsulation holds the core actives stable, and a liposomal form that mirrors the structure of skin carries them further in.

PROMISE

Balance comes from sequence

Loading in more good ingredients is not what changes skin. What decides the result is the state the skin is in, and the order in which things go on.

So rather than list products, we designed a sequence: prepare the skin (Reset), deliver the actives (Boost), then hold that condition in place (Keep). Each stage has a technology of its own, so that every stage creates the conditions for the one after it.

HERITAGE

From Bespoke Formulation Skin Code

At our offline studio, COCODEMER used scientific skin diagnosis (CSTI) to sort skin into 16 types, coded the active ingredients, and compounded each product one to one. That service has since closed. The diagnostic framework and formulation logic it produced now live on in the 16 Skin Code Serums.
